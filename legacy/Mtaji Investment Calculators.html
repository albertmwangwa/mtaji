Uses actual NAVs: units bought at the sale price on the date you pick, valued at the 12 Nov 2026 price. NAV only, so Bond Fund and Jikimu payouts are not included.

Projections assume the return you set is earned evenly every month. A single year's return can be far above or below the long-run average, so for long horizons try a lower rate as well. Unit trust NAVs move up and down, and past growth does not promise future growth. Top-ups buy units at the sale price; the exit charge is the gap between sale and repurchase price published by UTT AMIS. Other managers do not publish a separate repurchase price, so no exit charge is applied to their funds; their management fees are already inside the NAV returns shown.

DSE listed shares

Closing prices for 13 Nov 2026 from the DSE. 1W compares with the 6 Nov close; 1M is the change over the last 20 trading sessions. Dividend yield is the latest declared dividend (DSE dividend records, 2025 or 2026) divided by today's price, before the 5% withholding tax; for other shares, enter the latest dividend in the calculator yourself. Cross-listed shares (X) are Kenyan companies that also trade on the DSE. Select a share to load it into the calculator.

Trading costs follow the DSE fee schedule: brokerage of 1.7% on the first TZS 10M of a trade, 1.5% on the next 40M and 0.8% above 50M, plus 18% VAT, then DSE 0.14%, CMSA 0.14%, Fidelity Fund 0.02% and CDS 0.06% (plus VAT). That is about 2.38% for a trade under TZS 10M, charged on both buying and selling. Brokers can charge less on large trades. Dividends from DSE-listed companies carry 5% withholding tax. Price growth is an assumption you set: DSE shares can fall as well as rise, and some counters trade rarely, so selling at the price shown is not guaranteed. Dividends are assumed to be paid once a year at year end.

Data used. UTT AMIS sale and repurchase prices and fund sizes are official NAVs for 12 Nov 2026. Bond Fund payouts come from its interim report to 31 Dec 2025 and its June 2024 annual report (TZS 1.00 a unit a month). Other managers' NAVs, 1M / 3M / 12M returns, sizes, fees, minimums and redemption times are as listed by NAVView TZ for 3–6 Nov 2026. IEACLC-ETF uses its NAV for 10 Nov 2026. M-Wekeza uses the SanlamAllianz Pesa yield as a stand-in. DSE share prices are the official closes for 13 Nov 2026 (dse.co.tz), with 6 Nov closes from the DSE weekly report and 1-month changes from Kanza Capital Partners' DSE record. Dividends come from DSE and company notices; trading costs from the DSE fee schedule. NAV history uses audited half-year NAVs from UTT AMIS interim reports and financial statements (Jun 2023 to Dec 2024), UTT AMIS daily publications from 13 May 2025 to 30 Dec 2025, and 2026 points derived from period changes published by NAVView TZ. Treasury yields marked BOT come from Bank of Tanzania auction results from September to early November 2026 (the 11 Nov 10-year result was not yet published); those marked estimate are placeholders. Replace any figure with the latest auction or NAV before you rely on it.
These calculators are for planning. They are not investment advice, and returns from unit trusts are not guaranteed. Sources: uttamis.co.tz, dse.co.tz, navviewtz.com, itrust.co.tz, bot.go.tz.
